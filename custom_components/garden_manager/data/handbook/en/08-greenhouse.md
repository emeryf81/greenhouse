# The greenhouse

A greenhouse extends the season by weeks at both ends and makes heat-loving crops (tomato, pepper, aubergine, cucumber, melon) reliable in a cool climate. Under glass, though, you are responsible for what nature does outside: water, air and temperature.

## Temperature

| Crop | Night minimum | Day maximum |
|---|---|---|
| Tomato | 10 °C | 30 °C |
| Pepper, aubergine | 12-13 °C | 32 °C |
| Cucumber | 14 °C | 32 °C |
| Lettuce, spinach (winter) | 2 °C | 20 °C |

Above 30 °C tomato flowers drop (pollen becomes sterile). Hortulus warns when the greenhouse gets hotter than its crops tolerate and creates a **ventilate** or **shade** task.

## Ventilation

- Open vents once it reaches 22-25 °C inside, even on cloudy days.
- Close in good time in the evening to keep warmth in spring and autumn.
- Ventilation also removes humid air: the main defence against grey mould and mildew. Hortulus calculates **disease risk** from greenhouse humidity.

With a vent opener (cover, switch or fan entity) Hortulus can ventilate and shade automatically in *auto* mode.

## Shading

From late April the sun can heat a greenhouse above 40 °C within an hour. Shade paint or 30-40 % shade netting prevents scorch. Remove it in September for autumn crops.

## Water and feeding

- No rain falls in a greenhouse: watering is always needed, even after a wet day.
- Preferably in the morning, never over the leaves.
- Greenhouse soil is depleted and salted up faster: add compost every year and flush thoroughly in winter.

## The greenhouse year

- **February**: spring clean - wash the glass (every 10 % less light costs growth), disinfect staging, check vents.
- **April-May**: put up shading.
- **September**: remove shading, sow autumn and winter crops (lamb's lettuce, winter purslane, spinach).
- **October-November**: after the last harvest empty everything, **clean and disinfect**, refresh the soil or let it rest.
- **November**: fit bubble insulation, check heater and min/max thermometer.

Hortulus puts these tasks in the calendar automatically for every greenhouse or cold-frame zone.

## Cold frame

A cold frame is a small, low greenhouse. Ideal for hardening off, early lettuce and radish, and overwintering. Prop it open during the day, close at night.
