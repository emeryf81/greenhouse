# Pests

A healthy garden always has some pests - and the natural enemies that live on them. The goal is balance, not a sterile plot.

## Prevention

- **Rotation** and correct spacing.
- **Biodiversity**: flowers (French marigold, calendula, phacelia, dill) attract hoverflies, lacewings and parasitic wasps.
- **Insect mesh** over brassicas and carrots is the most effective protection against cabbage root fly, cabbage white and carrot fly.
- **Check** regularly: Hortulus schedules checks for susceptible crops.

## The main pests

| Pest | Crops | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Aphids | nearly everything | Blast off with water, earwigs/ladybirds, not too much nitrogen |
| Slugs | lettuce, young plants | Collect at night, beer traps, ferric phosphate pellets, hedgehogs |
| Cabbage white | brassicas | Insect mesh, pick off caterpillars |
| Cabbage root fly | brassicas, radish | Collars around stems, mesh |
| Carrot fly | carrot, parsnip | Mesh (60 cm high), onions nearby, thin on still days only |
| Onion fly / leek moth | onion, leek | Mesh, carrots nearby |
| Whitefly | tomato, cabbage | Yellow sticky traps, *Encarsia* wasps in the greenhouse |
| Red spider mite | cucumber, beans under glass | Raise humidity, predatory mites |
| Flea beetle | rocket, radish | Keep moist, mesh |
| Colorado beetle | potato | Pick off, remove eggs under leaves |

## Biological help

Under glass, **natural enemies** (parasitic wasps, predatory mites) work very well and are sold in small packs. Outdoors: leave a wild corner, hang an insect hotel, provide water for birds and hedgehogs.

## Avoid broad-spectrum sprays

Insecticides kill beneficials too, so the next pest outbreak is worse. Soft soap against aphids is an exception for emergencies.
