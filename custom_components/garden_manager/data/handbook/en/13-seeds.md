# Saving seed

## Which plants

Start with **self-pollinators**: tomato, bean, pea, lettuce. Their seed comes (nearly) true. Don't use **F1 hybrids**: their offspring don't resemble the parents.

Cross-pollinators (brassicas, squashes, carrot, onion, beetroot) cross with other varieties nearby and need isolation or hand pollination.

## How

| Crop | Method |
|---|---|
| Tomato | Ferment seed with pulp in a jar of water for 2-3 days, rinse, dry on kitchen paper |
| Bean, pea | Let pods dry on the plant, thresh |
| Lettuce | Cut the dry seed heads, shake out |
| Herbs (dill, coriander) | Let seed heads finish drying in a paper bag |

## Storing

- Cool, dry and dark; ideally a glass jar with a sachet of rice or silica gel in the fridge.
- Label species, variety and year.
- Viability: onion and parsnip 1-2 years, carrot and lettuce 3 years, tomato, bean and brassicas 4-6 years.

## Germination test

Put 10 seeds on damp kitchen paper in a closed box at room temperature. After the normal germination time count how many sprouted: 8 out of 10 = 80 % germination. Sow more thickly when it's low. Hortulus includes the germination test in the yearly *Check seed stock* task.
