# Diseases

Most vegetable diseases are **fungi**. Their spores only germinate on a wet leaf at moderate temperatures. Keep leaves dry and you prevent most of them.

## How Hortulus assesses risk

- **Leaf-wet hours**: hours with relative humidity of 90 % or more at 10-25 °C in the next 48 hours.
- **Hutton criteria** for late blight (potato and tomato): two consecutive days with a minimum temperature of at least 10 °C and at least 6 hours of humidity above 90 %. That is the weather in which blight explodes.
- **Greenhouse**: humidity above 85 % raises the risk, above 92 % even more.
- A **leaf wetness sensor** reading wet right now raises the level.

At moderate or high risk Hortulus creates an inspection task for susceptible crops (nightshades, cucurbits).

## Main diseases

| Disease | Crops | Signs | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| Late blight | potato, tomato | Brown patches with a pale edge, brown stems | Remove affected leaves, tomatoes under cover, resistant varieties |
| Powdery mildew | courgette, squash, pea | White powder on leaves | Airy spacing, water in the morning, spray milk 1:9 |
| Downy mildew | lettuce, onion, spinach | Yellow patches, grey fuzz below | Wider spacing, don't wet the leaves |
| Grey mould (botrytis) | strawberry, tomato, lettuce | Grey fluffy mould | Ventilate, remove dead leaves |
| Clubroot | brassicas | Swollen roots, wilting | pH > 7, rotation of 6+ years |
| Blossom end rot | tomato, pepper | Black patch at the fruit base | Even watering (calcium transport) |

## In general

- Water **at the base** and in the morning.
- Generous spacing and ventilation under glass.
- Diseased plants **not** on the compost heap.
- Choose resistant varieties.
- Disinfect tools after working on diseased plants.
