# The garden year and winter rest

Hortulus ties all seasonal work to your frost dates. Below is the year for a temperate climate (last frost around late April, first frost late October).

## Winter (December - February)

- **Plan**: layout, rotation, order seed.
- **Tools**: clean, sharpen and oil.
- From February: **clean the greenhouse**, set up the sowing station, first sowings indoors (pepper, aubergine, celery).
- Leave the soil alone: don't walk on or work it when wet or frozen.

## Spring (March - May)

- Turn and sieve the compost.
- **Prepare beds**: loosen, weed, add compost.
- Sow outdoors from March (broad beans, peas, spinach, radish), carrots from April.
- Tomatoes and other tender crops go out after the Ice Saints.
- Shade the greenhouse.
- Mulch once the soil has warmed.

## Summer (June - August)

- Water, weed, pinch out and tie in tomatoes.
- Harvest - often, so the plants keep producing.
- Sow again for autumn and winter: endive, lamb's lettuce, winter purslane, spinach, Chinese cabbage.
- Sow green manure on every bed that falls empty.

## Autumn (September - November)

- Harvest and store (see *Harvest and storage*).
- Remove greenhouse shading; autumn and winter crops under glass.
- **Clean and disinfect the greenhouse** after the last crop.
- Start a compost heap with leaves.
- **Drain hoses, drip lines and water butts** before the frost.
- Protect tender perennials and potted herbs.

## Winter rest

The soil rests in winter but should not lie bare:

- Cover it with **green manure** (sown in August-September) or a **mulch** of leaves, straw or compost.
- Leave hollow stems and seed heads: beneficial insects overwinter in them.
- Don't dig in autumn; frost and worms do the work.
- In the greenhouse: let the soil rest or replace the top 20 cm, ventilate now and then against mould.

Hortulus schedules *Put the garden to bed for winter*, *Cover bed for winter* and *Prepare greenhouse for winter* automatically at the right time.
